# Listening stats

The **Stats** tab in the History visualizes your listening habits.

## Range selector

The dropdown in the top right corner lets you select the time window for the top lists and activity charts. The listening calendar always covers the last 12 months.

## Widgets

- Top lists: This section shows three charts: top artists, top albums, and top tracks
- Time of day: A circular clock chart shows total listening time broken down by hour of the day
- Day of week: A bar chart shows the average daily listening time for each day of the week over the selected range
- Listening calendar: Like the well known Github chart, this one shows you a grid of days of the last 12 months, and colors them according to your activity on each day.

### Multi-artist tracks

When a track has more than one artist, each artist is credited the full listening time for that track. If you listen to a 4-minute track by two artists., both artists gain 4 minutes in your history.
